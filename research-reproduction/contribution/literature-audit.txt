PRIMARY-SOURCE REFERENCE REVIEW — 28 October 2026

The manuscript references were checked against publisher, official project,
NeurIPS/JMLR or arXiv pages. Preprints are identified as preprints; publisher
acceptance is not inferred from an arXiv identifier.

Baseline: Guruge and Priyadarshana (2025), Frontiers in Computer Science,
article 1509165. The prior baseline source audit and preserved original PDF
provide the detailed paper/code comparison.
https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1509165

Prophet: Taylor and Letham, Forecasting at Scale, The American Statistician
72(1), 37–45. DOI metadata uses the 2018 issue and 2017 online DOI.
https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2017.1380080

LSTM: Hochreiter and Schmidhuber, Neural Computation 9(8), 1735–1780 (1997).
https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.1997.9.8.1735

ACI: Gibbs and Candès, NeurIPS 34, 1660–1672 (2021).
https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/0d441de75945e5acbc865406fc9a2559-Abstract.html
Online inference: Gibbs and Candès, JMLR 25(162), 1–36 (2024).
https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/22-1218.html
The rolling/clipped implementation here does not inherit their formal results.

BACC: Fan Liu, Behrooz Farkiani, Guanqi Li, Patrick Crowley.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20575v2
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20575v2
The version-2 full text dated 13 October 2026 includes request-latency as well
as CPU objectives. The abstract page still emphasizes CPU. The full text is
the basis of the manuscript's description. Service-risk profiling, calibrated
forecasts and budget feedback overlap substantially with the broad proposal.
Our frozen effective-P99 curve plus separate residual margin is a tested
engineering choice, not proof of a new general architecture. No BACC performance
comparison is claimed without running its system.

KARMA: Julien Soulé, Jean-Paul Jamont, Michel Occello, Louis-Marie Traonouez,
Paul Théron. Abstract/full author version describes a cluster-trace Twin,
simulation-trained agents and transfer to a cluster.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21559v1

SLO-Scaler: Shuo Wang, Xiaoxuan Sun, Shao-yu Huang, Bencheng Su, Shuo Xu,
Netra Awate. Description in our manuscript is limited to the primary abstract's
Bayesian prediction and dependency-aware scaling claims. Its full implementation
was not reproduced, and its numerical results are not copied into our tables.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18390v1

InferScale: Wen Huang, author version posted 25 October 2026. Its arXiv record
reports an IEEE CLOUD publication, but the manuscript cites the verified arXiv
author version rather than asserting independently checked publisher metadata.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34006v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34006v1

Official operational references:
https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/autoscaling/horizontal-pod-autoscale/
https://keda.sh/docs/2.18/scalers/external/

No blanket novelty, theoretical coverage, superiority over these systems, or
production reliability claim is supported by this literature review.
